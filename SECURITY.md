# Security policy — SebasTN Inc.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Email the product the report is about:

- Access0x1 (the payment router and its contracts): **hello@access0x1.com**
- ClickReserv: **hello@clickreserv.com**

Put "SECURITY" in the subject, and include the affected component, the steps to reproduce, and how you would like to be credited.

We acknowledge within 48 hours and agree a fix and disclosure date with you.

## Scope

In scope: SebasTN Inc.'s production sites and the Access0x1 contracts listed at [access0x1.com/mainnet](https://access0x1.com/mainnet).
Out of scope: third-party services (Stripe, cloud providers), social engineering and denial of service.
